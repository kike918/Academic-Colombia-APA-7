The page should explain:

- the authority hierarchy;

## License

The standard MIT License remains appropriate for the original project-authored skills, workflows and documentation because the project is intended to be reusable, forkable and adaptable across AI platforms.

A new `docs/LICENSE-SCOPE.md` clarifies:
- MIT does not grant repository write access;
- third-party and institutional materials keep their own rights;
- registry inclusion does not relicense external content;
- institutional names do not imply endorsement;
- contributors must have rights to submitted material.

Status: **GREEN**.
