## Current Gemini product facts verified

Official Gemini help was checked during this release.

As of 2026-08-31:
- custom Gems can be created in the Gemini web app;
- a Gem accepts custom instructions;
- Knowledge files can be added from the device;
- Knowledge files can be added from Google Drive when the relevant Workspace/Activity connection is available;
- Drive-backed files can reflect later changes to those files;
- Gems created on web may be available in mobile/Workspace surfaces subject to product availability;
- Knowledge citations are a platform feature and can be disabled in supported settings.

Official references used for product behavior:
- https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15146780
- https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15235603

No fixed file/token limit is made part of Academic Colombia's contract because product limits may vary/change.
