# v0.14 — Final Empirical Validation

## Purpose

This report records the last planned empirical-validation batch before v1.0 release preparation.

The batch adds:
- a real native UNAD accounting XLSX;
- a 14-page accounting visual PDF;
- a 13-page coffee-sector macroeconomic visual PDF;
- a 10-page inflation/IPC UNAD presentation with a video reference.

Private source artifacts and personal identifiers are not committed.

## Major result

The strongest new evidence is the native XLSX execution.

The workbook demonstrates a real formula-linked path:

`journal → T-accounts → trial balance → SIIGO reconciliation`

Observed results:
- 14 journal blocks balanced individually;
- workbook formula-error scan found no common spreadsheet formula errors;
- final debit and credit totals both equal COP 117,860,000;
- SIIGO detailed voucher reports the same totals;
- inventory/cost-of-sales arithmetic reconciles to COP 23,772,000;
- trial balance was rendered and visually inspected.

This promotes native XLSX empirical coverage from fixture-only to executed.

## Why negative findings matter

The visual cases demonstrate that Academic Colombia should not behave as a presentation beautifier.

### Coffee-sector macro deck

The deck is visually strong but contains material evidence/semantic problems:
- `-28%` production is directionally supported by official FNC `-28.3%` year-to-date data;
- `6.9 million sacks` is misused as the size of the drop even though the official source uses it as the current coffee-year production volume;
- an export contraction of `-15%` lacks period/source reconciliation against official values that differ materially by period;
- `2.5 million fewer sacks` lacks transparent derivation in the inspected source;
- several different shock types are grouped too broadly as negative externalities;
- traceability is said to eliminate information asymmetry rather than reduce/mitigate it.

Correct framework result: visually strong, academically `NOT READY` until the evidence and conceptual findings are fixed.

### Inflation/IPC presentation

Official DANE April 2026 figures validate:
- Colombia annual IPC `5.68%`;
- Bogotá annual IPC `5.60%`.

However, a year-end 2026 projection around `3.9%` lacks precise source/date/forecast-type traceability and is inconsistent with the direction described in Banco de la República's April 2026 policy report.

Correct framework result: current data sample PASS, forecast evidence gate FAIL/PARTIAL.

### Accounting visual deck

Core accounting concepts are broadly correct, but:
- the sale diagram should distinguish the revenue/cash entry from the cost-of-sales/inventory entry;
- absolute language such as technology `guaranteeing` trust should be bounded;
- no visible bibliography/source page was observed in the inspected export.
